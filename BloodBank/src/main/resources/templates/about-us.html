About LifeLine

We connect generous donors with the patients who need them, quickly and safely.

LifeLine Blood Bank started from a simple observation: in an emergency, the blood a patient needs usually exists somewhere nearby — it just cannot be found in time. We keep a verified donor directory and a request queue that staff can act on within minutes instead of days.

Every blood request is reviewed by an administrator before it is approved, and every donation is recorded against the donor's profile so that eligibility gaps between donations are respected.

Built Around One Promise.

"Nobody should lose someone because the right donor could not be reached in time."

That promise shapes how we handle matching, verification and urgency. Requests marked critical move to the front of the review queue, and donors are only contacted about the blood groups they can actually help with.
